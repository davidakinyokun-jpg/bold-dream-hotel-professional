Your Privacy

Privacy Policy

How Bold Dream Hotel collects, uses and protects information provided through this website.

Last Updated: August 30, 2026

Bold Dream Hotel respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using our website.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our website, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Room type
  • Check-in date
  • Check-out date
  • Number of guests
  • Messages or other information submitted through our booking or contact forms

We may also collect basic technical information about your visit, such as your browser type, device information and general website usage information, where applicable.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information you provide may be used to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and messages.
  • Process and manage booking requests.
  • Contact you regarding your booking or enquiry.
  • Provide information about our rooms and services.
  • Improve our website and services.
  • Maintain the security and proper operation of our website.

3. How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information submitted through our website against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or misuse.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information.

Your information may be accessed by authorized members of Bold Dream Hotel or service providers that help us operate the website and process enquiries or bookings, where necessary.

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law or where necessary to protect the rights, safety or property of the hotel, our guests or others.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve functionality, understand website usage or support services such as analytics.

Where required, we will request appropriate consent before using non-essential cookies or tracking technologies.

6. Third-Party Services

Our website may contain or use services provided by third parties, such as Google Maps, analytics services, social media platforms or other website services.

These third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including the right to request access to, correction of or deletion of certain information we hold about you.

To make a privacy-related request, please contact Bold Dream Hotel using the contact details provided on our website.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is not specifically directed at children, and we do not knowingly request personal information from children for purposes unrelated to providing our services.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, services or legal requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact Bold Dream Hotel through the contact information provided on our website.
